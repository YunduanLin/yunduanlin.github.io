Good morning, and in case I don't see you, good afternoon, good evening, and good night!

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Decisions, Operations and Technology at CUHK Business School. I was very fortunate to be advised by Zuo-Jun Max Shen, whose mentorship shaped how I think about operations: a decision is rarely just one decision. A store location can redirect attention, a platform policy can change who gets seen, and an AI recommendation can reshape what people learn to do next. I build research around these chain reactions, with the goal of helping platforms, businesses, and public-service systems become more intelligent, scalable, and socially aware.

Research Interests

My work is about connected decision-making in operational systems. I focus on two streams that often talk to each other:

A small working philosophy: decisions have social lives. The systems I study are messy in useful ways, and the fun is to make their hidden structure visible enough to support better decisions.
Social network analytics and platform operations

What happens after one person clicks, shares, visits, adopts, or refers? I study how social ties, spatial proximity, and platform design turn local actions into market-level outcomes, with projects on recommendations, adoption, referrals, and content exposure.

peer influence platforms diffusion incentives
AI for operational decision-making

What should AI do inside a decision system: predict, simulate, advise, or step back? I study how AI changes the behavior of human experts, AI agents, and public-service systems when decisions are sequential, social, and consequential.
