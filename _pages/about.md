I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Decisions, Operations and Technology](https://www.bschool.cuhk.edu.hk/departments/decisions-operations-and-technology/) at [CUHK Business School](https://www.bschool.cuhk.edu.hk). I received my Ph.D. and M.S. from the University of California, Berkeley, and my B.Eng. from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/index.htm), all in Civil Engineering.

My research studies societal operations management, with a focus on social network analytics, platform operations, optimization, and data-driven decision making. I am especially interested in how networked systems shape adoption, engagement, and operational performance in digital platforms and other connected service systems.
